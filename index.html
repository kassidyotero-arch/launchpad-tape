Listening for swaps

Waiting for new Rhea swaps of tokens launched on Nearly or JustHoot. The last 100 trades are cached locally and roll over automatically.

Rolling tape: the last 100 trades are saved locally and roll over automatically as new transactions arrive. After a pause or dropped connection, the tape updates from the newest block.
Detection: a trade is any successful Rhea swap where one side is a token under *.nearlytrade.near (Nearly) or *.launchpad.justhoot.near / *.v2-launchpad.justhoot.near (JustHoot, including tokens still on their bonding stage). Market cap = trade price × ft_total_supply at the time the token was first seen.
NEAR value is exact when the swap touches wNEAR; marked est when it was priced through another token. USD uses the NEAR price when the trade arrived.
Fastest mode races all feeds at once: Intear, the on-chain decoder (reading blocks before finality when the block service allows it) and RAEN. The first to see a trade shows it; Settings shows which feed is winning.
Main feed: Intear (api.intea.rs, free, no key) pushes every trade on tokens under nearlytrade.near and both JustHoot launchpads (launchpad.justhoot.near, v2-launchpad.justhoot.near) as soon as its block lands. Fastest mode adds RAEN when you have a key; on-chain decoding takes over automatically if Intear drops.
Sources: Fastest (default) runs the on-chain feed and RAEN together and shows whichever sees a trade first; the copy from the other feed is merged, not shown twice. Hover an age to see which feed caught it and how many seconds after the trade it appeared. RAEN polls RAEN's decoded /trades feed every second (slowing down automatically if RAEN asks) with your key, which is kept only in this browser. On-chain decodes NEAR blocks yourself with no key; it only sees trades routed through Rhea. If RAEN refuses the key or is unreachable, the tape switches to on-chain automatically. Either way it starts at “now” and never shows past trades.
Free-tier friendly on-chain mode: one request per block (about 100 a minute, under neardata's 180/min limit), each held open by the server until the block is final, and blocks without a launchpad token are skipped without being parsed.
Data: blocks from mainnet.neardata.xyz (FastNEAR), token info from NEAR RPC, NEAR/USD from Binance with CoinGecko fallback, other token prices from the Rhea indexer. Transaction and wallet links open on Pikespeak. Click a token or wallet to filter by it; ✎ adds a private label to a wallet.
