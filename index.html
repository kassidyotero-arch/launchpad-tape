Listening for swaps

Loading the latest trades on Nearly and JustHoot… New trades appear here live as they happen.

Real-time first: the live feed starts at the newest block when the page opens and never streams in past transactions; on open, the latest 100 trades are loaded once as background and shown with their real ages. After a pause or a dropped connection it jumps to the newest block instead of catching up.
Detection: a trade is any successful Rhea swap where one side is a token under *.nearlytrade.near (Nearly) or *.launchpad.justhoot.near / *.v2-launchpad.justhoot.near (JustHoot, including tokens still on their bonding stage). Market cap = trade price × ft_total_supply at the time the token was first seen.
NEAR value is exact when the swap touches wNEAR; marked est when it was priced through another token. USD uses the NEAR price when the trade arrived.
Fastest mode races all feeds at once: Intear, the on-chain decoder (reading blocks before finality when the block service allows it). The first to see a trade shows it; Settings shows which feed is winning.
Main feed: Intear (api.intea.rs, free, no key) pushes every trade on tokens under nearlytrade.near and both JustHoot launchpads (launchpad.justhoot.near, v2-launchpad.justhoot.near) as soon as its block lands. Fastest mode adds the on-chain decoder racing alongside Intear; it takes over automatically if Intear drops.
Sources: Fastest (default) runs Intear and the on-chain feed together and shows whichever sees a trade first; the copy from the other feed is merged, not shown twice. Hover an age to see which feed caught it and how many seconds after the trade it appeared. On-chain decodes NEAR blocks yourself with no key; it only sees trades routed through Rhea. If Intear is unreachable, the tape switches to on-chain automatically. RAEN (optional, with your key, which is kept only in this browser) only supplies extra alerts in the Alerts bar; it does not feed trades. Either way it starts at “now” and never shows past trades.
Free-tier friendly on-chain mode: one request per block (about 100 a minute, under neardata's 180/min limit), each held open by the server until the block is final, and blocks without a launchpad token are skipped without being parsed.
Data: blocks from mainnet.neardata.xyz (FastNEAR), token info from NEAR RPC, NEAR/USD from Binance with CoinGecko fallback, other token prices from the Rhea indexer. Transaction and wallet links open on Pikespeak. Click a token or wallet to filter by it; ✎ adds a private label to a wallet.
